# Limitations

This AI-assisted research manuscript is offered for independent review, not as an institutional submission or a claim of completed external peer review. Confirmed GitHub identities are Drefetr and magna-nz; legal names and affiliations are not inferred. The AI-role account is in CONTRIBUTORS.md.

Finite-mode stability, finite-horizon identities and restricted identification retain their assumptions. No general nonlinear stationary exact-memory theorem, Wilks law, universal detection threshold, global optimizer certificate or physical inhibition discovery follows. The paper retains heavy-tail, nonregular-boundary, supplied-versus-estimated-nuisance, reused-reference/adaptive-development and observational limitations.

The earlier manuscript's original frozen catalogue, fitting implementation, complete logs and editable source remain unavailable. The reconstructed GeoNet snapshot is a separate input. This public distribution excludes third-party event records and the earlier PDF. Live reacquisition can differ from the historical snapshot. Full synthetic/reference banks and recovery states are not shipped, so a checkout cannot reproduce every historical campaign alone.

Dependency and build pins describe current public preparation. The historical TeX cache was not snapshotted, preventing exact historical byte/layout replay. Compilation, deterministic tests and visual inspection do not certify every theorem or historical computation. Optional GPU operations and full observed fitting were not rerun during preparation.

The separately dated F continuation retains all 2,149 identities but only 7,068 of 8,596 method outcomes are controlled. Its null-query rejection bounds span roughly .03 to .41 across procedures, so empirical null size and a size-matched power ranking remain unresolved. The 2,000 resamples describe sensitivity rather than established confidence coverage. The same 25 continuation pilot fits change decisions when the reference calibration changes; this is posthoc accounting, distinct from the historical eta-planning pilot. Geometry is partial and conditional. No further recovery or research cycle was run as part of this manuscript integration.
